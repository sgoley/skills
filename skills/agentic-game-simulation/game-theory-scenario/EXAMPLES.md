# Examples

## 1) Contract negotiation

**Scenario**: Vendor renewal with price increase and new exclusivity clause.

**Prepared pathways**:

- Cooperative: vendor trades exclusivity for longer term.
- Neutral: partial concession with tighter service-level terms.
- Adversarial: take-it-or-leave-it demand, requiring exit option.

**Readiness focus**: pricing anchors, fallback terms, and walk-away threshold.

## 2) Prospective job interview (candidate fit with team)

**Scenario**: Candidate has strong technical skills but unclear collaboration style.

**Prepared pathways**:

- Cooperative: candidate demonstrates strong cross-functional examples.
- Neutral: mixed signals require targeted follow-up questions.
- Adversarial: defensiveness under challenge suggests cultural mismatch.

**Readiness focus**: behavioral probes, conflict-handling prompts, and calibration criteria.

## 3) Relationship decision between two people

**Scenario**: Two partners deciding whether to relocate for one career opportunity.

**Prepared pathways**:

- Cooperative: shared criteria and phased timeline.
- Neutral: unresolved tradeoffs trigger delayed decision with checkpoints.
- Adversarial: value conflict requires boundary-based pause.

**Readiness focus**: values clarification, non-negotiables, and de-escalation language.

## 4) Critical HOA email thread

**Scenario**: Multi-party HOA thread escalating over budget and rule enforcement.

**Prepared pathways**:

- Cooperative: move thread to structured agenda and decision owner.
- Neutral: continue async with tighter framing and fact table.
- Adversarial: accusations escalate; move to moderated meeting format.

**Readiness focus**: neutral framing, stakeholder-specific concerns, and escalation controls.

## Output pattern (concise)

1. Scenario Snapshot
2. Evidence Dossier (emails/transcripts/notes/decisions + reliability + key quotes)
3. Actor Incentive Matrix
4. Pathway Tree (3-6 branches)
5. Response Menu (questions, reframes, boundary lines, fallback moves)
6. Risk / Escalation / Exit Plan
7. Rehearsal Drills

## Intake prompt examples for evidence

- "Please share any relevant email snippets, especially moments where someone stated a boundary or preference."
- "If you have meeting transcripts or notes, paste the parts that reveal how each person argues or decides."
- "List prior decisions on similar issues, including who decided and the stated reason."
- "Share your own notes (sales CRM, prep notes, reflections) that capture stakeholder tendencies."
